What we found on the current site

Checked on linksatnormangolf.com on 7 October 2026. None of it is hard to fix; all of it costs you golfers.

  • The Book Tee Time button opens a date that has passed

    The link behind it is hard-wired to date=06/01/2026 with an old session code attached, so every golfer who taps it starts on June 1 and has to find today themselves. Some won't.

  • The 17th hole has the 16th's description

    Hole 17 is a 503-yard par 5, but its note reads "A great par 3. A par is all you want," copied from 16.

  • Hole 2 is labelled "Par 2"

    The course tour's second heading says "Par 2" where it should say "Hole 2".

  • The designer's name is spelled two ways

    "Lindy Lindsey" in one place and "Lyndy Lindsey" on the amenities page.

  • One photograph of the course

    The whole site has a single picture of the golf. The lake, the 13th, the cedar holes: none of it is shown, so nobody can see why it's worth the drive.

  • No hours and no green fees

    A golfer deciding where to play today can't see either, so they call or they go somewhere that shows them. (We haven't put these on the new site either; we'll add them when you give us the numbers.)

The plan

About two weeks from a yes, most of it waiting on your answers.

  1. Pick a design

    A, B, or a mix. Tell us what to change; we make it before you decide anything else.

  2. Fill in the gaps

    Hours, green fees and twilight rates, any league or event you want shown, and any photos you have of the course.

  3. Move the address over

    We point linksatnormangolf.com at the new site, keep your email working, and fix the tee-time link everywhere it appears.

  4. Launch and check

    We test it on phones and desktops, submit it to Google, and walk you through changing anything later.
