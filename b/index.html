A yardage book for the Links.

Eighteen holes on an old sheep farm three miles from the University of Oklahoma, routed by Lindy Lindsey through cedar and elm and around a twelve-acre lake. Bentgrass greens, Bermuda fairways, four sets of tees, and a clubhouse with two pools and a gym for the family that came along.

On the course

Everyone signs in at the pro shop and starts at the first. Collared shirts and soft spikes. Carts are required, on the path unless the 90-degree rule is posted; drivers are sixteen or older. Golfers under fourteen play with an adult.

After the round.

The Links is a golf and athletic club. The clubhouse holds the pro shop, the Grill, men's and ladies' locker rooms and an activity room with billiards and tanning beds. Outside are a resort-style pool with a cabana, a wading pool with a fountain for small children, a whirlpool and a sauna. The fitness center has cardio machines and a full weight room. All of it is on the one family membership.
