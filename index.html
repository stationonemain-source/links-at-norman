      Golf in Norman, Oklahoma

      The Links at Norman is an 18-hole, par-71 championship golf course in Norman, Oklahoma, less than three miles from the University of Oklahoma. Designed by Lindy Lindsey, it plays 6,519 yards from the black tees and 4,833 from the red, with bentgrass greens and Bermuda fairways, tees and roughs. The course is routed through cedar and elm trees around a 12-acre lake, and the 13th — the signature hole — wraps around it.

      The Links Golf & Athletic Club adds a resort-style pool, a wading pool for children, a fitness center with cardio and weight training, a whirlpool and sauna, locker rooms, a pro shop and the Grill. Family memberships are $125 a month on a 12-month contract or $1,500 a year, plus tax, and include unlimited golf with no green fees.
